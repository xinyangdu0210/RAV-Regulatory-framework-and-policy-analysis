A policy-first deployment roadmap

The sequence keeps procurement from outrunning authority, safety evidence, community design, or operational controls.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Define service and authority

    Public purpose · lead agency · jurisdiction · funding conditions
  2. 02

    Bound

    Set the rural ODD

    Roads · weather · speed · connectivity · fallback
  3. 03

    Assure

    Build the safety case

    Compliance · validation · incidents · reporting · exemptions
  4. 04

    Include

    Design the complete service

    Accessibility · booking · assistance · privacy · public engagement
  5. 05

    Operate

    Authorize a measurable pilot

    Roles · training · metrics · stop rules · independent review
  6. 06

    Learn

    Publish and decide

    Results · incidents · equity · costs · scale, revise, or stop

Method and limitations

Built for traceable policy planning

Policy records and links will be added only from federal and state bill sources supplied by the project owner. The website does not generate or select policy sources automatically.

Policy changes quickly. Confirm current requirements with the responsible agency and qualified counsel before procurement, testing, or deployment.
